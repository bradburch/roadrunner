What Roadrunner does

Roadrunner adds the species you logged on eBird and iNaturalist to the Strava activity they happened during, with counts from eBird checklists and names from iNaturalist observations.

  1. It reads your most recent Strava activities and your latest observations from the nature apps you've linked.
  2. It matches them by overlapping date and time.
  3. For each match, it writes a “Nature seen during activity” block into the Strava description. Existing text is kept, and re-syncing never duplicates it.

Only eBird checklists with a recorded duration can be matched; incidental observations without one are skipped. iNaturalist observations need a recorded time to be placed in an activity.
