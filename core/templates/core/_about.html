How it works

Three steps, then go outside

  1. Connect Strava

    Sign in with Strava so Roadrunner can read your recent activities and update their descriptions.

  2. Link your nature apps

    Add your eBird profile ID, your iNaturalist username, or both. Synced activities merge species from every source you link.

  3. Record as usual

    Roadrunner matches sightings to activities by overlapping date and time, then writes a “Nature seen during activity” block into the description.

Only eBird checklists with a recorded duration can be matched; incidental observations without one are skipped. iNaturalist observations need a recorded time to be placed in an activity. Your eBird profile must be public.
