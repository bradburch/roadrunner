Features

What Roadrunner does

Connect once and keep logging the way you already do. Roadrunner handles the rest.

Automatic on upload

When a new activity reaches Strava, Roadrunner checks it against your sightings right away. You don't need to press anything.

Catches late logs

Submitted your checklist after the drive home? Roadrunner checks again 2, 4 and 8 hours after the upload to pick it up.

eBird checklists with counts

Each species from an overlapping checklist is listed with the number you recorded.

iNaturalist observations

Plants, insects, reptiles, fungi: anything you observed during the activity is listed by name. All it needs is your username.

Keeps your words

Your existing description stays put. The species block is added below it and updated in place, so re-syncing never duplicates it.

Sync anytime

Linked a new source or fixed a checklist? Hit Sync now to re-check your recent activities on demand.
